### 2. What we believe

**H2:** Clarity is a design decision

Great design brings clarity through simplicity. Every screen we ship has to earn its place: if something does not help a person understand, decide or act, we remove it.

We work with ambitious teams to turn complex ideas into digital products that are intuitive, purposeful and distinctly modern. Not modern for a season. Modern because the thinking underneath is sound.

**Pull quote (quotable standalone line):**
> A good digital product does not ask people to work it out. It already has.

---

### 3. How we work with you

**H2:** A small senior team, on purpose

We move with the focus and speed of a small senior team. The people you meet in the first call are the people who design, build and ship your product. Nothing is handed down a chain.

We approach every project with three priorities:
- **Precision.** Every detail is considered, and anything that does not serve the product is removed.
- **Velocity.** Short loops, clear decisions and visible progress every week.
- **Longevity.** Work designed to stay relevant long after current design trends fade.

We partner with a limited number of brands each quarter. That limit is how we guarantee full focus on yours.

### 5. Our process

**H2:** A disciplined path to launch

1. **Discover.** We go deep into your brand, market, audience and competitors to uncover what they have missed.
2. **Strategize.** We build a precise roadmap: priorities, budget, messaging and milestones, before any build begins.
3. **Execute.** Designers, developers and strategists work as one team and ship with obsessive attention to detail.
4. **Optimize.** We track what matters, cut what does not work, scale what does, and report everything transparently.

### 8. FAQ

**How big is the Bongo Expert team?**
We are a small senior team by design. The people who scope your project are the people who design and build it, which keeps decisions fast and quality consistent. For larger launches we bring in trusted specialists, and the core team stays responsible end to end. `[confirm team size; needs source]`

**What kind of projects do you take on?**
We design and build websites, mobile and web apps, and AI-powered product features, and we work on brand identity when a product needs one. We work best with ambitious teams who want a long-term product, not a quick template. We take a limited number of projects each quarter.

**How long does a typical project take?**
It depends on scope. A focused marketing website usually takes 6 to 10 weeks from discovery to launch, and apps and AI products take longer. You get a clear timeline and weekly milestones after the Discover and Strategize phases. `[confirm ranges with the team]`

**Do you work with clients outside your country?**
Yes. We work remotely with teams in different time zones and plan overlap hours for reviews and decisions. Every project has one shared roadmap, so progress is visible wherever you are. `[confirm]`
